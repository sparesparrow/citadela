## Three Value Propositions

### I. Exclusivity — *An address that answers only to you.*
Citadela is small on purpose. A limited collection of suites, a single concierge who knows your name before you arrive, and a shoreline that, for the length of your stay, feels like a private estate. Nothing is shared that you did not choose to share.

### II. Nature — *Moravia, distilled to water and light.*
Forest folds down to the water's edge; the reservoir holds the sky like polished bronze. Here the loudest sound is the morning. You do not visit the landscape at Citadela — you wake inside it, behind glass, above the still surface of Brněnská přehrada.

### III. Freedom — *The shoreline, on your own current.*
Every stay includes a private electric scooter, charged and waiting. Trace the water silently at dawn, vanish into the pines by afternoon, return whenever the light tells you to. No timetable, no engine, no trace — only the open shore and the quiet pull of going further.
