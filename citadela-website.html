Why Citadela

Quiet luxury, kept for a few.

I

An address that answers only to you

Citadela is small on purpose. A limited collection of suites, a single concierge who knows your name before you arrive, and a shoreline that, for the length of your stay, feels like a private estate.

II

Moravia, distilled to water and light

Forest folds down to the water's edge; the reservoir holds the sky like polished bronze. You do not visit the landscape here — you wake inside it, behind glass, above the still surface of the přehrada.

III

The shoreline, on your own current

Every stay includes a private electric scooter, charged and waiting. Trace the water at dawn, vanish into the pines by afternoon, return whenever the light tells you to. No timetable. No engine. No trace.

The Arrival

Cross the threshold of the everyday.

You are met at the gate, not the desk. A glass of something cold and Moravian, the day's plan dissolved into possibility, your scooter already charged at the water's edge. The road narrows, the trees close in, and then the reservoir opens beneath you like a sheet of dark gold. From this moment, the only schedule is your own.
