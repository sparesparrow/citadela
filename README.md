# CITADELA — Brand Identity

*Premium stay & private electric-scooter experience — Rozdrojovice · Brněnská přehrada · Jižní Morava*

**Tagline:** *Gold light on dark water.*

A complete luxury visual identity in matte black and rich gold. Open
**`citadela-brand-guidelines.html`** first — it presents the whole system on one page.

---

## What's inside

### `citadela-brand-guidelines.html`
One-page brand book: logo suite, colour, typography, motifs, voice, and the poster — self-contained, open in any browser.

### `01-logos/`
The mark is a geometric art-deco citadel (stepped towers, crenellated crown) over three fading water ripples — drawn as outline paths, so it's resolution-independent and needs no fonts installed. SVG (vector, for print/scaling) + PNG (1600px, for quick use).
- `citadela-horizontal` — primary lockup
- `citadela-stacked` — vertical lockup (+ `-transparent`)
- `citadela-icon` — rounded tile for apps / avatars
- `citadela-glyph` — bare mark, no ring

### `02-identity/`
- `citadela-pattern.svg` — gold citadel lattice for backgrounds
- `citadela-wax-seal.svg` — embossed wax medallion
- `citadela-card-front/back.svg` — business card
- `citadela-letterhead.svg` — A4 letterhead
- `preview-*.png` — rendered previews of each

### `03-poster/`
- `citadela-poster.png` — art-deco travel poster (2000×3000)
- `citadela-poster.svg` — editable vector source
- `DESIGN-PHILOSOPHY.md` — the "Gilded Stillness" rationale

### `04-website/`
- `citadela-website.html` — full luxury site (hero, Arrival, Suites, The Journey, Reserve). Self-contained; open in a browser. The reserve form is a styled demo — it confirms on screen and captures nothing.

### `05-copy/`
- `citadela-web-copy.md` — all website copy: tagline, hero, three value props, section text, footer.

## The system at a glance

**Colour**
`#0A0A0A` matte black (ground) · `#C9A84C` rich gold (accent) ·
`#E7CE84` gold light · `#9A7E32` gold deep · `#F3ECDD` cream white (text)

**Type**
Italiana — logotype & display · Playfair Display — headlines · Jost — body & interface

**Voice**
A five-star concierge who already knows your name: confident, unhurried, never loud.

---

*A note on imagery:* the website and poster use original gold-on-black SVG art rather than
licensed photography, to keep the package free of copyright encumbrance. The art plates are
built to be swapped for commissioned photography when available.
